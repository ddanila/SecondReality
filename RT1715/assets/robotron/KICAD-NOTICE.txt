Modifications by the DAC project, 2026-10-11:
- tessellated STEP geometry using CadQuery 2.5.2 / OCP 7.7.2;
- centered each package in X/Y, retaining its original board plane and scale;
- split the fused solid at its plastic side walls into body and lead meshes;
- retained the design exception for these derivatives.
No intentional change to the outer package shape or dimensions was made.
See tools/convert-kicad.py, sources.json and models.json for reproduction,
source hashes, mesh hashes and geometry bounds.

The models identify physical package families, not the exact DDR manufacturer,
mould revision or electrical function of every photographed chip. Pin-count
matches, placement and orientation are provisional where the reference photo
is ambiguous. No unverified electrical part numbers or resistor values are added.
Board images remain separately credited to Old Crap Vintage Computing. They
show another PC 1715, not Danila's exact 1715M/W motherboard.

The derived models.json footprint coordinates are measured from STEP lead
sections at the seating plane by tools/convert-kicad.py. They retain the
corresponding source model licence, like the derived tessellated meshes.
